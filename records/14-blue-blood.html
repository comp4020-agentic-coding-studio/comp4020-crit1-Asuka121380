The Review

I saw a photograph of X in a Japanese music magazine before I heard a single note. The hair suggested theatre, perhaps comedy, but a shop assistant assured me the band played with genuine force and put “Kurenai” on the store system. The quiet introduction gave me no warning for the speed that followed. Blue Blood is excessive in every direction: fast metal, romantic piano, enormous ballads, and a visual sense that refuses ordinary scale. That excess is precisely why I like it. “Kurenai” remains the essential track, while “Endless Rain” demonstrates how comfortably the band can exchange aggression for sentiment. The album is long, and not every dramatic gesture deserves its full running time, but restraint would make it a different record. My copy includes a booklet crowded with photographs and Japanese text. Even when I cannot follow every word, the conviction needs no translation.
