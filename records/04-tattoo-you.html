The Review

This cassette lived in the glove box of my first unreliable car, and the plastic shell is now scratched from years of sliding around with loose coins. “Start Me Up” was the obvious reason to buy it, a riff so confident that even a failing car stereo could not weaken it. These days I prefer the quieter second half, especially “Waiting on a Friend.” There is something reassuring about hearing the Stones step away from swagger and simply wait on a doorstep. “Slave” also has that loose, after-midnight groove they can find without seeming to count the bars. The album is not perfectly unified, and at times you can hear that its pieces came from different moments, but I have stopped treating that as a defect. It feels like a box of old photographs that happens to contain several excellent ones. Mine still has a petrol-station price sticker on the case, and I refuse to remove it.
