The Review

By this point I trusted Fishmans enough to order an album without hearing a note. The parcel arrived with a customs crease across one corner, and I waited until the house was quiet before playing it. 宇宙 日本 世田谷 feels both domestic and enormous, as if ordinary streets open directly into space. “Walking in the Rhythm” is my favourite because its repetition never stands still; voice, bass, and percussion keep adjusting the air around the same forward motion. “Weather Report” and “Magic Love” offer clearer shapes, but the album’s real strength is the atmosphere connecting them. It is less immediate than Orange and less singular than Long Season, yet perhaps more inhabitable than either. I do not understand every lyric, so I resist inventing meanings where tone is enough. This became a record for solitary walks and late work, a companion that never demands conversation but always alters the pace of the room.
