The Review

I resisted The Smiths because the most devoted fans at school treated misery as a competitive sport. Then a girl I knew put “There Is a Light That Never Goes Out” on a mixtape between two much heavier songs, and the combination of melodrama and genuine tenderness got through my defences. I bought The Queen Is Dead second-hand and discovered that Johnny Marr’s guitar was far more muscular and inventive than the band’s reputation suggested. “Bigmouth Strikes Again” moves beautifully, while “I Know It’s Over” can still make an empty room feel much larger. Morrissey occasionally tests my patience; a clever line can turn into a performance of cleverness. Even so, the album balances wit, loneliness, irritation, and desire better than most records attempting only one of those things. My copy has no inner sleeve, so I made one from brown paper and wrote the track names by hand.
