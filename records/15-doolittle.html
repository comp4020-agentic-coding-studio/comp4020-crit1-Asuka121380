The Review

I heard “Monkey Gone to Heaven” on community radio and went looking for the band with the strange name and the singer who seemed to be arguing with the microphone. Doolittle was difficult to find locally at first, so my original copy was a home-recorded cassette sent by a friend in Melbourne. When I finally bought the CD, “Debaser” became the song I played first: a blunt guitar entrance, a ridiculous shout, and then two minutes of perfect control pretending to be chaos. The quiet-to-loud changes have since been borrowed by half the alternative bands in my cabinet, but the Pixies still sound less calculated than their followers. “Here Comes Your Man” is almost suspiciously friendly, while “Gouge Away” ends the album with real menace. The lyrics remain a collection of films, animals, religion, and private jokes. I do not need to solve them. The sound tells the story well enough.
