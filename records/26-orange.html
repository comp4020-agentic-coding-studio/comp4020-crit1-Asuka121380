The Review

Fishmans entered my collection through a badly labelled tape passed around by friends interested in Japanese dub and pop. The song I later identified as “MELODY” sounded loose enough to fall apart, yet every bass movement and echo arrived exactly where it was needed. Finding Orange required several letters, an international money order, and more patience than buying music ought to require. That effort probably made the first proper listen more ceremonial than the album expected. Compared with the later Fishmans records, this one is brighter and more immediate, still connected to a band playing together in a room. “MELODY” is my favourite, but the entire record has a warm, slightly crooked pulse that suits late afternoons. Shinji Sato’s voice may initially seem fragile beside the rhythm section. I came to hear that fragility as the centre of the music. This CD began a small obsession that now occupies several precious centimetres of shelf.
