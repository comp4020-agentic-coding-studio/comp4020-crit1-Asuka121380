The Review

John Frusciante’s return made me curious, but “Scar Tissue” made the purchase unavoidable. I first heard it on a small radio at work, and even through the weak speaker the guitar had an exposed, sun-bleached quality unlike the crowded attack of the earlier records. Californication is more reflective than Blood Sugar Sex Magik, although the band still cannot resist bouncing off the walls when an opening appears. “Scar Tissue” remains my favourite, particularly the short solos that say enough and leave. “Otherside” carries real weight, and “Road Trippin’” ends the album without requiring a final explosion. There are too many songs, and the compressed sound can become tiring at high volume. I would happily remove one or two lesser tracks to give the strongest more room. Still, the record captures a band returning from damage without pretending recovery is complete. My copy spent one summer permanently beside the living-room player.
