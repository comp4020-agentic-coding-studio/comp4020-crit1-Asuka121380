The Review

I discovered The Square through the same small circle of fusion collectors who introduced me to CASIOPEA. One of them played “Truth” at unreasonable volume and challenged me to name the instrument carrying the melody. I guessed guitar, then keyboard, before admitting that the bright electronic wind instrument had completely fooled me. The title track is immediate and joyful, built for movement, but the musicians never treat accessibility as an excuse to relax their standards. The album sometimes approaches the polished edge where fusion can begin to resemble expensive background music. Just when that worry appears, a rhythmic turn or solo snaps everything back into focus. I ordered the CD under the T-Square name and later learned that the band was still called The Square when the album appeared, a tiny catalogue detail I enjoy explaining to unwilling visitors. It is excellent music for driving, cleaning, or recovering from a gloomy record.
