The Review

I had filed Radiohead under “promising band with one enormous song” until a shop assistant played “Just” over the speakers and pointed at The Bends without saying anything. The guitars seemed to bend the room around Thom Yorke’s voice, and the songs carried more unease than the polished surfaces first revealed. “Street Spirit (Fade Out)” is my favourite because it closes the album without offering a comfortable exit; that repeating guitar figure feels inevitable from the first note. “Fake Plastic Trees” can be painfully beautiful, though I have heard enough poor acoustic imitations of it to last a lifetime. “My Iron Lung” provides the necessary bitterness toward the success that nearly trapped them. The record is dramatic, occasionally too eager to prove its sensitivity, but its emotional scale feels earned. My copy accompanied several long bus journeys and has circular scratches from a portable player that objected to every pothole between Canberra and Sydney.
