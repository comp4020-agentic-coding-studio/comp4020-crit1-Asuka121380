The Review

(live) My father liked the Eagles, so for years I treated them as respectable furniture rather than a band I might choose myself. The reunion television broadcast changed that. The acoustic opening of “Hotel California” was familiar but newly tense, and the long closing guitars made three generations in our lounge stop talking. I bought Hell Freezes Over soon afterward. The new songs are uneven, though “Get Over It” has enough irritation to justify the reunion, and “Love Will Keep Us Alive” is smoother than I generally prefer. The live performances are the reason the disc remains here. They are polished almost to the point of caution, but the singing and guitar arrangements reveal why these songs survived years of radio repetition. I still reach for rougher music more often. This album has another purpose: it records one of the rare evenings when my father and I agreed about what should be played next.
