The Review

After the rough workshop feeling of Homework, the brightness of Discovery surprised me. “One More Time” was everywhere, but it was “Digital Love” that convinced me. I heard it in a friend’s car at night, and the guitar-like solo made the old distinction between rock instruments and electronic construction seem especially pointless. The song is joyful without being empty, carrying the awkward hope of a dream that ends too early. “Aerodynamic” joins a baroque figure to a hard rhythm with complete confidence, while “Something About Us” provides the quiet centre I did not expect. At times the album’s shine approaches childhood television and advertising, which may repel listeners who require electronic music to remain severe. I think the openness is brave. The record treats remembered pop sounds as raw material for a future that, from 2001, still seems friendly. I keep the Daft Club card inside even though I never made proper use of it.
