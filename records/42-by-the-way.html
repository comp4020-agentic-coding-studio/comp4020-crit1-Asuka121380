The Review

This is the newest record in the cabinet and the reason the update date at the top of the site can no longer pretend it is still the nineties. I ordered the CD after hearing the title track on the radio, expecting another mixture of sharp funk and Californian melancholy. Instead, John Frusciante’s layered guitars and harmonies often guide the album toward pop, folk, and something almost dreamlike. “Dosed” is my favourite because the overlapping guitar lines and voices create a tenderness the band’s reputation would never have predicted. “Can’t Stop” supplies the necessary rhythmic snap, while “Venice Queen” earns its long closing position. The middle of the album is crowded, and a few songs blur together before their individual shapes emerge. I prefer that generosity to a safe repetition of earlier successes. After more than a decade of following this band, it is satisfying to hear them become softer without becoming smaller.
