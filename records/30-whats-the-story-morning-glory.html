The Review

By the time this appeared, Oasis no longer needed anyone’s private endorsement. “Wonderwall” escaped from radios, shops, parties, and every beginner’s acoustic guitar. I grew tired of it, then recovered after avoiding it for several months. The album’s true attraction for me is its sense of a night growing larger than the people inside it. “Champagne Supernova” is the favourite: nonsense and melancholy stretched across guitars that arrive like weather. “Some Might Say” carries the rough optimism of the first album, while “Cast No Shadow” briefly lowers the voice. The record is less urgent than Definitely Maybe and sometimes too pleased with its own importance. Even so, the melodies are undeniable, and the sequencing turns a collection of singles into a shared public memory. My sister gave me this CD for Christmas 1995, then spent the afternoon complaining that I played the closing track twice before lunch.
