Update Log

This page has never had a full redesign, so it's grown in patches over five years. This log explains why the collection spans two decades of releases while the layout has stayed more or less the way I first got it working. Newest entries at the top.

03 AUG 2003 Added Red Hot Chili Peppers — By the Way. Newest record in the cabinet, and the reason this page can no longer pretend it's still the nineties. See catalogue entry 42. 19 JUN 2003 Guestbook is working again after whatever the last host did to it. Old entries recovered from a backup, should all still be here. 25 JUL 2000 Two more Daft Punk records finally worth writing up properly. Fixed some entries that only had a title and no notes. 22 MAY 1999 The collection now runs to over thirty records catalogued properly. Thank you to visitor number 5,000 — sign the guestbook if that was you. 29 APR 1999 Traded emails with someone in Osaka about Fishmans imports. Added notes to a few of the Japanese entries while I was at it. 17 NOV 1998 Added a proper section for Japanese records once the import shop opened nearby — CASIOPEA, X Japan, Mariya Takeuchi and the rest finally got the write-ups they deserved instead of one-line entries. 14 MAR 1998 Site went online. First dozen or so records up, no images yet, and the visitor counter broke twice in the first month before I worked out why.

A note on dates: a few of the earlier entries were written up well after the record itself was first catalogued, so "logged" dates on individual record pages don't always line up neatly with when I actually bought the thing — that story is in the write-up itself.
