About This Page

I started buying records properly in the early 80s — mostly vinyl, whatever the local shop had in, plus a good stack of tapes traded with friends through school. By the 90s it was mostly CDs, though I never really stopped picking up the odd secondhand vinyl when I found it.

I learned a bit of HTML in 1998 from a library book and a lot of view-source, and put this page up not long after. It has never been redesigned. I don't have a strong reason for that beyond not wanting to lose the layout I got working the first time.
