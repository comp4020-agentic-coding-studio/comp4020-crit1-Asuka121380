About Me

I won't put my full name up here — there's no shortage of cranks on the web and I'd rather not make it easy — but if you've found this page you probably already know who runs it. I was born here in Australia around 1970, so I'm early thirties as I write this, and I've been buying records since I was about twelve.

It started with vinyl and home-taped cassettes off the radio and off friends' collections, because that's what there was. Through the eighties I picked things up wherever I could: garage sales, the school fete, whatever the local shop had in that fortnight. By the early nineties it was mostly CDs, though I've never really stopped keeping an eye out for secondhand vinyl, and I still trade tapes with a couple of old friends who moved interstate.

My favourite shop for years was a secondhand place two suburbs over that smelled like dust and cardboard and always had a progressive-rock crate nobody else was looking through. I've also relied a fair bit on mail-order and import catalogues for the Japanese records — postage costs more than I'd like to admit, but there's no other way to get some of this stuff locally.

Most of the listening in this house happens on an ordinary stack system in the front room, cassette deck on one side, CD player on the other, turntable underneath gathering more dust than it should. Nothing is particularly expensive and nothing here is precious in a way that stops me actually playing it.

I learned enough HTML in 1998 from a library book and a lot of view-source to put this page up, mostly so I'd stop losing track of what I owned. It has never been redesigned, and at this point I think I'd rather add records than rebuild the layout. If you want to talk about any of it, sign the guestbook or check the links page for where else to find me.
