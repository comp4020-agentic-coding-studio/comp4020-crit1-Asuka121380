Rock, Metal & Britpop

The loud shelf. Classic rock through grunge through Britpop — the records that get played when the volume knob matters.

AC/DC — Back in Black (1980)

Vinyl · acquired 1991, secondhand at a garage sale

Favourite track: “Back in Black”. Sleeve's a bit worn but it still plays clean. Cranked this the day I got my license and it's still my go-to when the car needs some noise.

Guns N’ Roses — Appetite for Destruction (1987)

Cassette · acquired 1988, traded two Iron Maiden tapes for it

Favourite track: “Sweet Child o’ Mine”. Tape's a bit stretched around track four but I can't bring myself to replace it.

Metallica — Metallica ‘The Black Album’ (1991)

Cassette · acquired 1991, bought at the mall the week it came out

Favourite track: “Enter Sandman”. Wore two copies of this tape out on repeat that summer.

Nirvana — Nevermind (1991)

CD · acquired 1993, record fair in Melbourne

Favourite track: “Come as You Are”. The one album on this shelf everybody's heard of. Doesn't make it any less good.

Oasis — (What’s the Story) Morning Glory? (1995)

CD · acquired Christmas 1995, gift from my sister

Favourite track: “Champagne Supernova”. Still don't fully understand the lyrics. Doesn't matter.

Red Hot Chili Peppers — By the Way (2002)

CD · acquired August 2003, mail-order catalogue

Favourite track: “Can’t Stop”. The newest thing in the cabinet — see the update log.
