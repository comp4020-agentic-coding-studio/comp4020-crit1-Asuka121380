Experimental & Progressive

The records that don't sit still — prog, atmospheric, psychedelic, and a couple of things I still don't have a genre for.

King Crimson — Discipline (1981)

Vinyl · acquired 1994, secondhand shop downtown

Favourite track: “Frame by Frame”. Took me three listens before it clicked. Now it's the one I recommend to anyone who says prog is boring.

Pink Floyd — The Division Bell (1994)

CD · acquired 1995, birthday present

Favourite track: “High Hopes”. Good for late nights with the lights off. Not their most famous record but it's the one I actually reach for.

Radiohead — OK Computer (1997)

CD · acquired 1997, bought the week it came out

Favourite track: “Paranoid Android”. Confused me the first time through. Still finding new bits in it six years later.

Fishmans — Uchū Nippon Setagaya (1997)

CD · acquired 2000, mail-ordered after a friend's tape dub

Favourite track: “Walking in the Rhythm”. A friend dubbed me a cassette of this years before I found the real CD. See also the Japan shelf.

Neutral Milk Hotel — In the Aeroplane Over the Sea (1998)

CD · acquired 1999, ordered through a fanzine classifieds page

Favourite track: “Holland, 1945”. Still not entirely sure what it's about. Plays it anyway, most weeks.
